**How to use these:**
- **Pick by content type first, then by mood.** Harnoor's explainers and AI/tool breakdowns are **Educational**. Personal story hooks are **Storytelling**. Behind-the-scenes or event vlogs are **Day in the Life**.
- **These are copyrighted songs, so never use them in sponsored posts or paid partnerships** (Bluehost, AMD, Travelopod…). For those, use an Epidemic Sound track that fits the same content type. For organic posts, add the song as Instagram music in the app at upload rather than baking it into the export.
- Use an instrumental version under the voice where one exists, about −24 dB.
- End it naturally (see the main README's **Ending background music**).
