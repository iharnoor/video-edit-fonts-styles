## Video direction

- Canvas: #EBEAE7 with a 24px dot grid (#000 @ 7%). Every visual is a floating card (white or #1C1C1E terminal), radius 18px, soft shadow `0 10px 40px rgba(0,0,0,.10)`.
- One accent: #FF5A1F. Used only for: dashed selection boxes, copy badge, target circle, chart spike, small labels, arrows.
- Captions: Inter 700, #2A2A2A, 64px, 1–3 words, in the lower third above the Shorts UI safe zone (y 1240–1420). Key word highlighted orange.
- Camera: the whole canvas is one big board; frames are regions of it. Transitions are camera glides/whips across the board with 6–10px motion blur, not crossfades.
- Pacing: a new visual beat every 1.5–2.5s (faster than the 16:9 reference, which is ~2–4s).
- Sound design: every card entrance = pop/click; text typing = ticks; moves = whoosh; big reveals = riser → 0.15s silence → impact. Bed sits ~-22 LUFS under the voice; SFX ~-14 to -18 dB peaks; voice always on top.
