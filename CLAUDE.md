# Video edit fonts & styles

This repo holds Harnoor's favorite fonts, pairings, motion styles and the graphics projects built from them. See [README.md](README.md) for the full catalog.

## Main style: warm & witty (default)

**Every new video uses Harnoor's main style, "warm & witty", unless Harnoor asks for something else.** Load the project skill [.claude/skills/warm-witty-style/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/warm-witty-style/SKILL.md) before any edit, graphics, captions or motion work. It holds the full spec: palette, type, caption style, signature moves, pacing, sound, grade and deliverables. The reference build is [videos/travelopod/](videos/travelopod/).

In short:
- Make it visual, not text-heavy. Prefer **animated generic UI cards that act out the line** (fare ticker, itinerary, calendar, call screen, lock screen, comment box) over word pills. Put them in the chest band under the chin, timed to the payoff words; captions sit above the head.
- **No sticky notes or wordy text asides.** Show the aside as a small drawn visual (icon or mini illustration, label of 3 words at most). Keep every card or board to about 6 words besides numbers and sources. The drawing kit is `.claude/skills/warm-witty-style/kit/illus.py`.
- Captions are lowercase Poppins ExtraBold with the key word in a sky-blue pill, above the head.
- Use a full-screen paper board whenever Harnoor reads off-camera.
- Trim pauses over 0.3s.
- Keep SFX sparse: they mark named things and section changes, never plain cuts.
- Cut on the words, then beat-align the music bed to the cut. Epidemic Sound tracks for sponsored posts.
- End the music naturally, never with a hard cut. Use one of the three endings in the README's **Ending background music** section: stem cut, reverb tail, or a riser + whoosh stack.
- In FCP, the grade goes on an **adjustment layer** ("Look Grade" title plus the Custom LUT effect) on lane 1, with visuals above it.
- Deliver an FCPXML, a LUT, an SRT and a preview.

## Premium checklist (applies to every style)

The README's **[What makes it premium](README.md#what-makes-it-premium)** section holds the shared rules measured from the RPN and Riley references (Nov 9, 2026), and every style skill has a **Sound** and a **Premium polish** section that applies them. Read both before an edit. In short:
- Show the real page, app or post being named; drawn visuals are for ideas with no real screen.
- Voice at −14 LUFS, range ≤3 LU on shorts and ≤5 LU on long form, levelled before the music goes in.
- One music bed at one level: 15–20 LU under the voice on shorts (22 LU for sponsors); on long form, hook and outro only and nothing under demos.
- Each style has a 3–5 sound SFX palette and a cap per minute. No sound on plain cuts, zooms or caption changes.
- Entrances settle within 10 frames, one thing moves at a time, and anything to be read holds 1.5 s.
- One accent colour, three type sizes, grade on the face only.
- End on a frame worth a screenshot.

- **Catch in-sentence restarts (every edit).** ASR keeps both halves of a restart inside one sentence ("Now, when you run Claude Code, okay, when you run open source model…"). Cut to the last restart. Also drop throat-clearing tails that only point elsewhere ("That is the big crux of it. If you want to go technical, you can read this whole blog."). Harnoor trimmed both by hand on the free-claude-code long form.
- **Cuts never clip a word (every edit).** End each piece at least 120 ms after the ASR word end, then run on to the next quiet point (100 ms at the noise floor). Start at the last quiet point before the onset. Pacing comes from removing gaps between phrases, never from shaving words. Check t/s/d/k/ch endings, numbers and "percent" on a waveform sheet. Reference: `.claude/skills/podcast-clips/kit/pieces.py`.
- **SFX come from Ocular Sounds first.** Harnoor's premium library is `~/Music/Ocular_Sounds/` (Foundation, Utility, Velocity, Shift, Mechanica, Swell, Pendulum, Cityscapes, Rainforest). Search each pack's `Metadata Import.csv` by tag instead of guessing filenames. Its loops and stems can also make a music bed when Harnoor wants Ocular-only music.
- **Referenced posts and clips go on screen.** When he says "this is the clip/post from X" and it isn't in the screen recording, capture the post (see the third-party rule) and show it for that line. On free-claude-code he had to add the @AlexFinn post himself (a screenshot, 14.5 s, about 82% scale, left of the corner cam).
- **Mac screen recordings: audio starts late.** QuickTime/macOS screen recordings can start their audio track ~0.1 s after the video (`ffprobe` stream `start_time`). A WAV cross-correlation gives the audio offset; add the audio `start_time` to get the video offset (free-claude-code: 138.65 + 0.10 = 138.75 s). On these VFR files put ffmpeg's `-t` before `-i`; an output `-t` after `-ss` wrote an empty file.
- **FCPXML times sit on the frame grid.** Snap every `offset` and `start`, b-roll source starts included, to the 1/30 s grid. Otherwise FCP warns "not on an edit frame boundary" for 23.976, 29.97 or 59.94 sources and for their connected children. Validate against FCP's own `FCPXMLv1_14.dtd`. Inside an `asset-clip`, `chapter-marker`s go after the connected clips, or the DTD check fails.
- **Never look copied.** A style learned from a creator gives the *structure*. Every edit uses Harnoor's own surfaces: palette, cards, chapter and title treatments, motion and wording. List what changed from the reference in `project.md`. For Riley, use the **Desk Notes** look (light or dark) in [riley-style/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/riley-style/SKILL.md).
- **Face cards and badges (every style).** Centre the face using Apple Vision tracking per piece, with the whole head inside and eyes about 40% down. If it won't fit, grow the card; never stretch the picture. Put the card on the side the face looks toward, so the gaze points into the content. Don't mirror the camera to fix the gaze. Check stills at 5+ points.
- **Fact-check before building.** Check every claim against its source first. Keep loose lines with an on-screen note, cut unsupported ones, and drop a reel whose point rests on an unsupported claim. **On long form he prefers a pickup or a cut over a note** (free-claude-code, Nov 27, 2026: he turned off 7 of 12 correction notes, re-recorded the model-name and "reuses layers" lines as a camera pickup and a voice memo, and cut the section whose demos came from a different model). So write the exact replacement lines for each wrong claim in `project.md` for him to record, and keep notes only for a name (lower third), a money figure or a price the viewer needs: 4 or fewer per long form, 2–6 s each. Where b-roll is from a different product than the line says, cut the claim; if the b-roll stays, one plain line on it (his: "this is 600B Inkling Small not mini"). Credit real photos of public figures from official or news sources, and never put private people's faces on screen.
- **Post and tweet cards stay whole (every style).** Show the full header (avatar, name, verified badge, @handle, date) and the full text, scaled to fit, never cropped to a 2-line fragment. Centre the card in its area. Mark the key words. Don't add a big view-count stat; at most keep the small native count. Use only the source tags accuracy needs, and no meta labels like "illustration" or "recreation" on obvious drawings.
