# Video edit fonts & styles

This repo holds Harnoor's favorite fonts, pairings, motion styles and the graphics projects built from them. See [README.md](README.md) for the full catalog.

## Main style: warm & witty (default)

**Every new video uses Harnoor's main style, "warm & witty", unless Harnoor asks for something else.** Load the project skill [.claude/skills/warm-witty-style/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/warm-witty-style/SKILL.md) before any edit, graphics, captions or motion work. It holds the full spec: palette, type, caption style, signature moves, pacing, sound, grade and deliverables. The reference build is [videos/travelopod/](videos/travelopod/).

In short:
- Make it visual, not text-heavy. Prefer **animated generic UI cards that act out the line** (fare ticker, itinerary, calendar, call screen, lock screen, comment box) over word pills. Put them in the top band, timed to the payoff words.
- **No sticky notes or wordy text asides.** Show the aside as a small drawn visual (icon or mini illustration, label of 3 words at most). Keep every card or board to about 6 words besides numbers and sources. The drawing kit is `.claude/skills/warm-witty-style/kit/illus.py`.
- Captions are lowercase Poppins ExtraBold with the key word in a sky-blue pill.
- Use a full-screen paper board whenever Harnoor reads off-camera.
- Trim pauses over 0.3s.
- Keep SFX sparse.
- Beat-align the music bed: Epidemic Sound tracks for sponsored posts.
- In FCP, the grade goes on an **adjustment layer** ("Look Grade" title plus the Custom LUT effect) on lane 1, with visuals above it.
- Deliver an FCPXML, a LUT, an SRT and a preview.

## When making video graphics

- Pull fonts and styles from this repo first: the pairings in the README, the font files in `fonts/`, the motion references under **Favorite motion styles**, and the REA explainer style.
- **Alternative look: editorial.** When Harnoor asks for the editorial, magazine or "premium" look, load [.claude/skills/editorial-style/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/editorial-style/SKILL.md) instead of warm & witty. It uses serif captions, paper cards, one oxblood accent, quiet eased motion and a film LUT.
- **Alternative look: dark cinematic.** When Harnoor asks for the dark, cinematic or moody look, load [.claude/skills/dark-cinematic-style/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/dark-cinematic-style/SKILL.md) instead of warm & witty. It uses frosted black glass cards over the shot, white Helvetica with one amber accent, light sweeps, blur-ins, parallax drift and a teal/warm film LUT with vignette and grain.
- **Alternative look: Proof Stack.** When Harnoor asks for Proof Stack or the "fav style.mp4" example look, load [.claude/skills/proof-stack-style/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/proof-stack-style/SKILL.md) instead of warm & witty. The face opens and closes; the middle is full-screen b-roll where real logo tiles, papers, polaroids, lanyard badges and browser windows land one by one and stack up in a dark, defocused space, with small white sentence-case captions and an occasional italic serif accent word.
- **Alternative look: Split Stage (Nick Saraev style) — Harnoor's favourite.** When Harnoor asks for "my fav style", "my favorite", the Nick Saraev style or Split Stage, load [.claude/skills/split-stage-style/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/split-stage-style/SKILL.md) instead of warm & witty. A light stage fills the top of the frame with real pages as blue-outlined browser windows, marker highlights and magnified callouts; the face sits low underneath, with big 1–3 word SF Pro captions (key word in blue Playfair italic), dark full-screen stat beats and a green glowing "comment" CTA. On split shots, use the **Kallaway crop** by default (and whenever Harnoor asks for it): the camera in a rounded card at the bottom, the head popping out above the card with the background removed (see the skill).
- **Alternative look: Dark Stage.** When Harnoor asks for Dark Stage, "the mix", "split stage x proof stack" or "like the Kimi reel", load [.claude/skills/dark-stage-style/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/dark-stage-style/SKILL.md). It puts Split Stage's visuals (Kallaway card, blue-outlined windows, stat numbers, green CTA) on Proof Stack's dark floor, with Proof Stack's small white captions.
- **Alternative look: Minimal Proof.** When Harnoor asks for Minimal Proof, "minimal captions", the Looki style or the Anthropic residency style, load [.claude/skills/minimal-proof-style/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/minimal-proof-style/SKILL.md). It uses lowercase Poppins captions on a soft black pill above the head, real page crops in browser windows and real posts with yellow marker highlights under the chin, a paper dot-grid board for full-screen beats, a light warm LUT, an Epidemic bed ~19 LU under the voice and sparse CC0 Openverse SFX.
- **Long form (16:9) styles.** Long form never has captions and keeps loose cuts. Screen Share (screen recording + corner cam, `videos/we-need-to-talk/`) is the default; the README's **Long-form styles** gallery lists Light Stage, Paper Podcast and Night Desk-Typecraft. When Harnoor asks for Night Desk, "the Typecraft style" or a darker, punchier long form, load [.claude/skills/night-desk-style/SKILL.md](.claude/skills/night-desk-style/SKILL.md): dark-mode screen with a yellow-ringed portrait corner cam, punch-ins on cuts, keyword slams, chapter cards on a dark scanline wall and neon diagram boards.
- **Every named style gets a screenshot.** When a new style is added, put a representative frame from its reference build in `styles/screenshots/<style>.jpg` (540×960 for shorts, 960×540 `long-<style>.jpg` for long form) and add it to the README's **Style gallery** (or its **Long-form styles** table) and to the style's own section.
- **Alternative looks: the 20 animation styles.** Only when Harnoor wants something other than warm & witty, suggest 2–3 styles from the README's **20 animation style options** that fit the script's tone (clean, loud, tech, nostalgic or dimensional), and let Harnoor pick. If Harnoor doesn't pick, choose one and say which. Use a single style for the whole piece unless asked to mix them. Follow that style's "Build it with" recipe, then apply this repo's fonts, safe zones and delivery rules on top. The available styles are Swiss style, Kinetic type, Pop art, Flat 2D, Clay 3D, Glassmorphism, Y2K chrome, Synthwave, Risograph, Paper collage, Glitch, Particles, Neobrutalism, Bauhaus, Memphis, Vaporwave, Pixel art, Isometric, Low poly and Line art.
- **Use particle orbs some of the time.** In roughly one out of every three or four graphics projects, or whenever a script beat talks about AI thinking, searching, connecting or building, include at least one [Thinking Orbs](https://github.com/Jakubantalik/thinking-orbs) moment. Good spots: an "AI is working" beat, a transition between cards, a loader before a reveal, or a small orb next to a caption. Don't put orbs in every scene; they should stay a recurring accent, not the main look.
  - Match the state to the line: `searching` for scanning or reverse engineering, `connecting` for agents or networks, `solving` for a reveal, `listening` for voice, `working` as the default.
  - Orbs are monochrome. On the REA board use dark ink; in a full-screen dark scene use light ink. On green-screen overlays put the orb inside a white or dark card so it keys cleanly.
  - In HyperFrames, drive the orb engine (`src/engine/`, deterministic in `t`) from the timeline time. Don't use the React component's own clock, which won't render frame-accurately.
- Talking-head shorts are 1080×1920 at 30fps, timed to the FCP `.itt` captions. Keep green-screen cards out of the face area (roughly y 460–1060); use the top band (y 80–430) or chest band (y 1110–1580).
- Deliver finished graphics to `~/Movies/FCP/<name>-graphics/` as well as the project's `renders/` folder.
